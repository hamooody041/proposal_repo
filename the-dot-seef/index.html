OUR PHILOSOPHY

The Science of Coffee,
The Art of Baking.

Every single origin bean in our espresso bar is ethically sourced from high-altitude micro-farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and El Salvador. Our beans are roasted locally in small batches to preserve delicate flavor notes, then brewed precisely down to the micro-gram.

In parallel, our temperature-controlled pastry kitchen operates 24 hours a day. Using strictly imported Normandy French butter, we spend 3 days laminating dough to guarantee an unparalleled level of light, structured flakiness.
