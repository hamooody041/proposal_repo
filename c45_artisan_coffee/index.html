The C45 Story

Where Mystery Meets Precision Craftsmanship

Located in Bahrain's vibrant Seef District, C45 Artisan Coffee was founded on a simple passion: pushing coffee extraction to its highest art form. Every bean in our reserve is meticulously selected from ethical micro-lot farms around the globe.

Whether you are seeking a quiet space to savor a hand-poured V60, indulging in our signature artisan beverages, or grabbing your morning brew before work, C45 delivers a refined sanctuary with every cup.
