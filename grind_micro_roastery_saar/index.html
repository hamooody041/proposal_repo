Our Roastery Craft

Micro-Roasting in Saar with Uncompromised Precision

At Grind Micro Roastery, we believe every coffee origin tells a story. Hidden in the vibrant heart of Saar, our team carefully selects ethically sourced single-origin green beans from high-altitude estates across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

We roast in tiny 5kg batches, developing custom heat profiles that showcase the intricate fruit notes, floral aromas, and rich body natural to each crop.
